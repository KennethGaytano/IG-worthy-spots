IG worthy spots


Salcedo Makati

Salcedo Village is a vibrant neighborhood in Makati known for its restaurants, cafes, offices, and weekend market. It offers a mix of urban living, food, culture, and leisure.

I recommend Salcedo Village for its clean streets, modern buildings, cafes, and urban atmosphere. It is a great place for capturing aesthetic Instagram photos, especially during golden hour and around the Salcedo Weekend Market.


Alabang Westgate

Alabang Westgate is a modern lifestyle and dining destination in Muntinlupa. It features restaurants, cafes, shops, and open spaces with a relaxed urban atmosphere.

I recommend Alabang Westgate for its stylish surroundings and photo-friendly spots. It is a great place for Instagram photos, especially street shots, cafe photos, and evening pictures.


Parqal Mall

PARQAL Mall is a modern lifestyle destination in Parañaque known for its open spaces, restaurants, shops, and contemporary architecture. It offers a relaxed atmosphere for dining, shopping, and taking photos

I recommend PARQAL Mall for its modern design and aesthetic spots. It is a great place for Instagram photos, especially architecture shots, outdoor photos, and evening pictures.
